A letter I couldn't say aloud

Dear Sir Abrar,

I don't know if you ever realized how this story started for me.

In 2022, I saw you randomly in a corridor. I didn't even know your name. You were simply someone who caught my attention for reasons I couldn't explain.

Then in 2023, when you came to teach us, something slowly changed. I started noticing the little things — the way you spoke, the way you taught, your expressions, your presence.

And before I even realized it, your presence had started to matter to me.

One of my friends noticed it before I could admit it myself. She literally looked at me and said, “Aasia, tu pagal hogayi he.”

Later, she told me that she had a crush on you too. And maybe that's when I started understanding that what I was feeling wasn't just a random attraction.

Because mine slowly became deeper. The crush quietly turned into one-sided love.

I never expected anything from you when these feelings started. I simply found myself caring, noticing, remembering and feeling happy whenever your presence was around.

Maybe you will never know the complete story behind these feelings. Maybe you don't need to.

I just wanted you to know that somewhere along the way, you became a very meaningful part of a chapter of my life.

And whatever life brings next, I will always be grateful for the lessons, the memories and the person I became while carrying this feeling.

Thank you for being someone I could look up to. Thank you for the memories. And thank you for unknowingly becoming a beautiful part of my story.

With respect,
Aasia
