A Letter From My Heart

Dear Sir Abrar,

I don't really know where to begin, because there are some feelings that become difficult to put into words. Still, I wanted to try.

You may never realize how deeply some of your words, your presence, and the little moments have stayed with me. Things that might have seemed ordinary to you sometimes meant much more to me than you could imagine.

I have carried many thoughts in my heart that I never found the courage to say. Maybe because some feelings are easier to hide than to explain.

You became someone I could never simply see as “just another teacher.” Somewhere along the way, your presence became meaningful to me in a way that I cannot completely explain.

I am not writing this expecting anything from you. I don't want this letter to make you uncomfortable, and I don't want an answer.

I simply wanted you to know that there was someone who genuinely valued you, remembered your words, and silently cared more than she ever knew how to say.

Maybe some feelings are not meant to become stories. Maybe some people simply leave a beautiful mark on our lives and remain a quiet chapter in our memories.

Whatever the future holds, I will always be grateful for the impact you had on me.

And on this Teachers' Day, I just want to say:

Thank you, Sir.

With respect and a heart full of words,
Someone who will always remember you.

Some people teach lessons from books.
Some people become a lesson in life.
