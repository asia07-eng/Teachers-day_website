A Letter From My Heart

Dear Sir Abrar,

I don't really know how to begin this letter, because there are some things that are difficult to put into words. Maybe because they have been sitting quietly in my heart for a very long time.

Maybe you have no idea ke aap meri life mein kitne meaningful rahe hain. Aapke liye shayad kuch moments ordinary honge, kuch baatein shayad aapko yaad bhi na hon… but mere liye woh moments ordinary nahi thay.

I remembered them. I carried them with me.

Kabhi kabhi mujhe khud samajh nahi aata tha ke main aapko itna deeply kyun feel karti hoon. It wasn't just about seeing you or talking to you. It was the way your presence made certain days feel different.

The way a few words from you could stay in my mind for days. The little things that you probably never thought twice about became memories that I kept returning to.

And honestly, maine bohat kuch khud se bhi hide kiya. I tried to convince myself ke maybe I'm just overthinking, maybe these feelings will disappear with time.

But some feelings don't disappear just because we don't talk about them.

There was a time when I wished I could tell you everything. Phir mujhe realise hua ke har baat keh dena zaroori nahi hota.

Sometimes, you can care for someone quietly, respect their place in your life, and still choose not to ask anything from them.

Aapne ek baar kaha tha ke the love you have for me will cause you pain. I don't know if you remember those exact words, but I remember them.

And maybe a part of me understood what you meant, even when I didn't want to.

I know that life has its own boundaries, responsibilities and realities. I know that not every feeling has a destination.

And I'm not writing this letter to change anything, or to ask you for anything.

I just wanted you to know.

That somewhere, there was a girl who genuinely cared about you. Who noticed the little things. Who valued your words more than you probably realised. Who sometimes smiled because of something as simple as a memory of you.

Maybe someday these feelings will become nothing more than a quiet chapter of my life. Maybe I'll look back and think about how deeply I once felt.

But even then, I don't think I'll regret caring.

Because you taught me something that wasn't written in any book—

that sometimes a person can become important to you without even knowing how much space they occupy in your heart.

So on this Teachers' Day, I don't just want to say “Happy Teachers' Day.”

I want to say thank you.

Thank you for the lessons you taught me, the memories you unknowingly gave me, and for being a part of my life that I know I will remember for a very long time.

I don't know what place I'll have in your memories.

But I know what place you have in mine.

And maybe that's enough.

Happy Teachers' Day, Sir Abrar. 🌙

With a heart full of words
I could never say out loud,

Someone who will always remember you.
